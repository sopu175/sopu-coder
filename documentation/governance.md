# 🏛️ SopuCoder Governance

This document describes the decision-making process, roles, and project lifecycle of the SopuCoder ecosystem.

## 🎯 Principles

- **Openness & Transparency:** Development discussions, benchmarks, and model decisions happen openly in public repositories.
- **Reproducibility:** All published models must be reproducible through documented training recipes or Modelfiles.
- **Safety & Ethical Integrity:** Models must not be intentionally trained or tuned to produce malicious exploits, telemetry bypasses, or license violations.

## 👥 Roles & Responsibilities

- **Maintainers:** Review pull requests, oversee releases, manage dataset integrity, and set technical directions.
- **Contributors:** Propose improvements, submit datasets, test benchmarks, and expand documentation.
- **Community:** Use the models, report bugs, provide feedback, and share use cases.

## 📦 Distribution Policy

SopuCoder maintains three distinct distribution channels:
1. **GitHub:** Source code, Modelfiles, benchmark suites, and documentation.
2. **Hugging Face Hub:** Verified weights, GGUF quants, dataset cards, and LoRA adapters.
3. **Ollama Registry:** Ready-to-use versioned model packages for local developers.
