# 📜 SopuCoder Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the SopuCoder project and community a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of background, technical experience, identity, or perspective.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive and constructive behavior include:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and patience toward fellow community members.
- Offering constructive, technical, and respectful feedback on code, datasets, and models.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism and acknowledging mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall developer community and project integrity.
- Prioritizing code safety, data licensing compliance, and honest benchmarking.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, derogatory comments, trolling, or personal/political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Maliciously submitting contaminated, copyrighted, or poisoned training data.
- Disruptive conduct in project discussions, issue trackers, or community forums.

## Responsibilities & Enforcement
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate, fair, and corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Reporting Guidelines
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers via the community repository or email at `sopu175@gmail.com`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
